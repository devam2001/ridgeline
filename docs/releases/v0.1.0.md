First release of Ridgeline: a Freqtrade bot for Binance spot with paper trading, an honest research pipeline, portfolio risk controls and reporting.

**Paper money only.** The panel runs Freqtrade in dry-run mode with a fake wallet and cannot start a live bot. No strategy is eligible for real money yet (see below).

## Install

- **Windows:** download `Ridgeline-Setup-0.1.0.exe` below and run it.
  - It installs for your user only (no admin rights) with its own Python, Freqtrade and FreqUI.
  - Start **Ridgeline** from the Start menu; the panel opens at http://127.0.0.1:8090.
  - Run **Update market data** once before using Replay or Research. It downloads several GB.
  - The installer isn't code-signed, so SmartScreen may warn: choose *More info → Run anyway*.
- **Docker (servers):** `docker compose pull && docker compose up -d` uses `ghcr.io/devam2001/ridgeline:0.1.0`. See `docs/OPERATIONS.md`.
- **From source:** `pip install -r requirements.txt`. See the README.

## What's included

- **Control panel**
  - Live paper trading with a fake wallet you choose.
  - Fast historical replay.
  - The research gate checklist.
  - Risk controls with a kill switch.
  - A hash-chained audit log.
- **Paper vs replay** (the last go-live gate): replays the exact weeks a paper session ran and matches the trades one by one. It checks four things:
  - 28+ days of paper trading
  - 80%+ of trades matched
  - return within 2 points of the replay
  - fills within 0.3% per side
- **Research pipeline**:
  - point-in-time pair universes
  - in-sample / out-of-sample split and walk-forward windows
  - fee stress and bias checks
  - nine go-live gates
  - `--balance` to run all of it for small accounts
  - a lookahead check that also works for strategies that rank pairs against each other
- **Four strategies**: TrendRider4h, MomentumRotation1d (new candidate), EmaRsiMomentum, TrendDipBuyer1h.
- **Portfolio risk limits**, enforced in backtests too: 35% max position, 4.5% max open risk, 3% daily loss limit, 15% drawdown halt.
- **Reports**: investor-style tear sheets, and the case study as a research paper (PDF, attached).
- **Operations**: watchdog with Telegram alerts, Docker deployment, CI.

## Research results (out of sample, 2025-06-01 → 2026-10-10)

The market fell 32.8% (equal-weight basket of the same coins) in this period.

| Strategy | Return | Max drawdown | Gates |
|---|---|---|---|
| TrendRider4h | +17.3% | 9.8% | 8 / 9 (99 trades, needs 100) |
| MomentumRotation1d | +13.1% | 5.2% | 7 / 9 |
| EmaRsiMomentum | +5.1% | 18.1% | 6 / 9 |
| TrendDipBuyer1h | −17.8% | 19.2% | 4 / 9 |

- **100 AUD (≈65 USDT) accounts work.** Percentage results match the 1,000 USDT runs.
- **More risk didn't help.** Doubling the risk per trade added ~3 points of return and doubled the drawdown.

Details are in `RESEARCH.md` and the attached case study.

Backtests are not a promise of future results. This is software, not financial advice.
