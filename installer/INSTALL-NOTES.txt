Ridgeline - paper-trading bot, research pipeline and control panel

PAPER MONEY ONLY. The panel runs Freqtrade in dry-run mode with a fake
wallet. It cannot start a live bot, and nothing it does reaches your
exchange account.

After installing:
  1. Start "Ridgeline" from the Start menu or desktop. The panel opens at
     http://127.0.0.1:8090 in your browser.
  2. Paper trading works straight away: pick a starting balance and a
     strategy, then press Start.
  3. Replay and research need historical candles. Run "Update market data"
     from the Start menu once (it downloads several GB), then monthly.

Your files live in the install folder's user_data\ directory:
  - config.private.json: your own API credentials, created on first start
  - paper\: paper-trading sessions
  - data\: downloaded candles
  - audit\: the audit trail
They are kept when you upgrade or uninstall.

Backtests and paper results are not a promise of future profits. This is
software, not financial advice. Only ever risk money you can afford to lose.
